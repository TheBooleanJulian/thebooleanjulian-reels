# Motion brief: the quality bar for every reel

Applies to captions, graphics, footage and cuts. The look itself is in `signature.json`.

- **Show the idea, don't decorate.** A ring locks onto the thing being talked about; a line connects two things being compared; a number appears when it is said. No icons or captions that carry no meaning.
- **One thing moves at a time.** Move the graphics or the camera, not both fighting. Anything attached to a subject must stay locked to it while the image zooms (use the zoom mapping).
- **Give motion weight.** Text rises and eases out; rings draw on; dashed lines trace. Big things move slower than small labels. Don't give everything the same bounce.
- **Hold what must be read.** A line stays on screen long enough to read it twice at phone size. Faster passages can have faster captions, calm passages need air.
- **Design for the phone.** Text inside the safe zone (y 250-1560), clear of the right-hand buttons (x > ~800 between y 700 and 1500) and of faces. Strong contrast: dark gradient or stroke behind light text.
- **Cuts are quiet.** 12 ms audio fades at every cut, no clicks; keep breath and rhythm so it still sounds like a person.
- **Check the real render.** Pull frames at the busiest moments and at every transition; look for overlaps, clipped words, label collisions, stray glyphs. Don't claim a review that wasn't done.
- **Facts are sourced.** On-screen numbers come from EXIF, an ephemeris, or the creator's notes. Label calculated values as calculated.
